# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The owner and two or three friends: F1 fans who argue about penalties. They open Noted after a session, often on a phone, sometimes on a laptop the day after a race. They know the sport well, including the drivers, cars and corners, but rarely read the FIA documents or regulations.

## Product Purpose

Noted reads every FIA stewards' decision and answers the question fans actually ask: why this penalty, and was it consistent with the last time this happened? For each ruling it shows:
- the stewards' own words, with the reasons they cited highlighted;
- the earlier rulings most like it, and whether their penalties were the same, harsher or lighter;
- where the cars were on track, when timing data exists.

Steward mode lets a reader rule before the reveal.

Success: a friend settles an argument by sending a link to the ruling page.

## Brand Personality

Pit-wall sharp. Dense, fast and data-first, like an F1 timing screen: strong contrast, numbers aligned, colour that carries meaning (penalty severity, harsher or lighter, car identity), with no decoration. Confident and terse in voice; it quotes the stewards and doesn't editorialise.

## Anti-references

- A generic SaaS dashboard: card grids, KPI tiles, gradients, rounded "widget" boxes.
- Reddit-thread clutter: walls of comments, vote counts, noisy controls.
- Official F1 marketing gloss and betting-app neon are also out of character.

## Design Principles

1. **The source is the authority.** Every claim points at the stewards' own text or the FIA PDF; nothing shown rests on words they didn't write.
2. **Read it like a timing screen.** Scan first, read second: aligned columns, tabular numbers, colour as signal, the verdict visible at a glance.
3. **Honest about the machine.** Where a label or ranking is automatic, say so plainly; never dress a guess as fact.
4. **Rule before you're told.** Steward mode hides the answer until the reader commits, so the comparison means something.
5. **Phone first.** The common case is a phone just after a session.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: contrast of at least 4.5:1 for text, full keyboard use, and visible focus. Colour is never the only signal; severity and comparison also carry a word or symbol. Every animation has a `prefers-reduced-motion` alternative, and the replay can be stepped by hand.
